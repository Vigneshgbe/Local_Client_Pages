About us

Seven decades of cultural service

The Sinhala Cultural Institute, known as Sudarshi, began in November 1954 with the movement of Nalini Wickramasinghe. It has since served as a community home for Sinhala culture in Colombo.

This section is where your full story will be told: the founders, the milestones, the people who carried the work forward, and what the institute means to the community today.
