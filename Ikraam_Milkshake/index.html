Our Story

It started with one question: why does a milkshake have to look boring on a menu?

MILKSHAKE was built on a simple belief — dessert should feel like an event, not an afterthought. Every recipe is designed with the same philosophy behind this brand: bold, unapologetic, and made to be craved before it's tasted.

Born in Colombo, we're building MILKSHAKE one creation at a time. No shortcuts, no unnecessary theatrics — just thick, cold, honest milkshakes made to turn an ordinary moment into something worth remembering.

— Ikraam Lahireen (Founder)
